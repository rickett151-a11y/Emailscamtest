Spot the Scam

Can you spot the scam?

Each level drops five emails in your inbox. Some are fakes and some are real. Check them over, decide what to do with each one, and earn up to three stars.

  • CheckTap the sender's name, links and attachments to see what's really behind them. Nothing will open.
  • DecideTrust it, verify it another way, or report it to Helpdesk.
  • LearnEvery clue gets marked on the email with a short explanation.
  • Score100 points for the best call, 50 for a safe one. 450+ earns three stars.
All emails, people and links in this game are made up. Your progress is saved on this device.
